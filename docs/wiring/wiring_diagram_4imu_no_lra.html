Build notes

  1. Never wire multiple 0x68 IMUs in parallel — each sits behind its own mux channel.
  2. Keep mux→IMU stubs under 15 cm to avoid I2C glitches.
  3. Star ground per IMU; 0.1µF ceramic at mux and each IMU VCC, bulk 10–100µF on the 3V3 bus.
  4. Bring up incrementally: validate CH0 alone, then add CH1→CH3 (NUM_IMUS 1→4).
  5. Firmware selects channels with i2c.writeto(0x70, bytes([1<<ch])) then readfrom_mem(0x68, 0x3B, 6).
  6. Haptic stage no-ops (duty 0) with no LRA populated — expected.
